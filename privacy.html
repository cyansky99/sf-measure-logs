Privacy Policy for sf-measure-logs

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Purpose

sf-measure-logs is a personal research utility used to upload experimental measurement logs and related data files to the owner's Google Drive using rclone and the Google Drive API.

Google Drive Data Access

The application accesses Google Drive only for the purpose of creating, uploading, reading, updating, and managing files required for measurement data storage.

The application uses the Google Drive API with limited access intended for files created or managed by the application.

Data Storage

Measurement data is stored in the Google Drive account authorized by the owner. The application does not operate an independent server for storing Google user data.

Data Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

OAuth Credentials

OAuth credentials are stored locally on authorized research computers as required by rclone. They are not intentionally transmitted to third parties other than Google services required for authentication and Google Drive access.

Users

This application is intended for personal research use by its owner and is not offered as a public service.
